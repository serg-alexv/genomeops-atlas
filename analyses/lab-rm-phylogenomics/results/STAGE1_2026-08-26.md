## Hard quality policy

- NCBI RefSeq annotated assemblies only
- assembly levels: Complete Genome, Chromosome, or Scaffold
- MAGs, atypical assemblies, and multi-isolate assemblies excluded by NCBI query
- named species required
- current organism name must match the operational taxon group
- CheckM completeness >=95% where reported
- CheckM contamination <=5% where reported
- assemblies limited to <=100 contigs
- one best assembly per named species selected first, then additional strains selected round-robin by species
